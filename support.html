Support

We're happy to help. Most questions are answered below.

Contact us: email contact@oshi-messenger.com and we'll get back to you within 1–2 business days. Please include your device model and iOS version.
I forgot my passcode. Can you recover my photos?

No — and that's by design. Your photos are encrypted with a key that exists only on your device, and your passcode is never sent anywhere or stored in a way we can read. We have no backdoor and no access to your data, so we cannot reset your passcode or recover your vault. Please keep your passcode somewhere safe.

How does the decoy vault work?

In Settings you can set a second "decoy" passcode. Entering that code at the lock screen opens a separate, harmless vault instead of your real one. This gives you plausible deniability if you're ever pressured to unlock the app. Your real vault stays completely hidden.

What is the intruder alert?

If someone enters the wrong passcode three times in a row, Secret Vault silently takes a photo with the front camera and records the time, so you can see who tried to open your vault. iOS always shows its green camera indicator while the camera is active — that's a system privacy feature we cannot and would never disable.

Will importing delete my originals?

Only if you turn on "Delete Originals After Import" in Settings. When enabled, after a photo is safely encrypted in your vault, iOS will ask you to confirm removing the original from your camera roll. Nothing is deleted without that confirmation.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select Secret Vault, and choose to change or cancel. If you cancel, you keep Premium until the end of the current billing period.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open Secret Vault, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. As long as you're signed in with the same Apple ID you purchased with, your Premium access will be restored. Note that your encrypted photos themselves do not transfer between devices automatically.

Is my data backed up?

Your encrypted vault lives on your device. End-to-end encrypted cloud backup is on our roadmap. Until then, we recommend keeping copies of anything irreplaceable, because if you delete the app or reset the vault, the encryption keys are destroyed and the data cannot be recovered.
