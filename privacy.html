Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 June 2026

The short version: Secret Vault does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no servers. Your photos, videos, and passcodes never leave your device.

1. Who we are

Secret Vault ("the app", "we", "us") is developed by Hugo Moriceau. This policy explains how the app handles information. You can reach us at hugomoriceau@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

None. Secret Vault has no user accounts and no backend servers. We do not collect names, emails, contacts, location, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or device identifiers.

3. Your photos and videos

Photos and videos you import are encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM (via Apple's CryptoKit) and stored only in the app's private, backup-excluded storage. The encryption key is generated on your device and held in the iOS Keychain, configured so it is never synced to iCloud and never leaves the device. We have no ability to access, view, or decrypt your content.

4. Camera access

The app requests camera access solely to power the optional "intruder alert" feature, which captures a photo with the front camera after three failed passcode attempts. These photos are stored encrypted on your device and are never transmitted anywhere. If you do not enable this feature, the camera is not used.

5. Photo library access

The app requests photo library access only to let you choose which photos and videos to import into your vault, and—if you enable it—to ask iOS to remove an original from your library after it has been encrypted. We do not scan, upload, or index your photo library.

6. Face ID / Touch ID

Biometric authentication is handled entirely by iOS. The app only receives a yes/no result from the system. We never see or store your biometric data.

7. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple may process purchase information under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app uses Apple's StoreKit to verify your subscription status on-device.

8. Third parties

The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The only third party involved is Apple, for App Store purchases and the system services above.

9. Children

Secret Vault is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

10. Data retention & deletion

Because everything is stored only on your device, you are in full control. Deleting an item, resetting the vault, or deleting the app permanently destroys the associated data and encryption keys. This cannot be undone, and we cannot recover it for you.

11. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and post the new version on this page.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hugomoriceau@gmail.com.
