Privacy, by design

No accounts. No servers. No tracking. Just your photos, locked down.

🔒

AES-256 encryption

Every photo is individually encrypted with AES-256-GCM using Apple CryptoKit. On disk, your library is unreadable noise.

👤

Face ID & passcode

Unlock instantly with Face ID or Touch ID, or fall back to your private passcode. Your key never leaves the device's Secure Enclave-backed Keychain.

🕵️

Decoy vault

Set a second passcode that opens a harmless decoy vault — plausible deniability if you're ever forced to unlock.

📸

Intruder alerts

After three wrong attempts, Secret Vault silently photographs whoever is trying to get in and logs the time.

🧹

Auto-clean originals

Import in one tap, then remove the originals from your camera roll so nothing private is left behind.

🙈

Stays hidden

Privacy blur in the app switcher, flip-face-down to lock, and on-brand disguised app icons.
